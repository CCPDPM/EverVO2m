What is a Movement Snack?

A movement snack is a short (2–15 min), intentional physical activity slotted into the gaps of your day: between meetings, after lunch, before bed. Inspired by Atomic Habits: small consistent actions compound into lasting change far more reliably than sporadic epic sessions.
