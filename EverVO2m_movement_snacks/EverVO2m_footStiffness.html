Stop clawing towels. Lock your arch into a spring.

Towel curls train your toes to scrunch and brake. Fast interval propulsion requires isometric big-toe extension and arch stiffness via the Windlass mechanism—returning up to 17% of stance energy for free.

Diagnostic 01 · 10-Second Test

What happens when you try Toe Yoga barefoot right now?

Try 5 slow switches (Big Toe Up / 4 Down → Big Toe Down / 4 Up) with the ball of your foot flat.

Diagnosis — Intrinsic Overload / Ankle Compensation: Your calf and Achilles are compensating for under-recruited intrinsic foot muscles (abductor hallucis & flexor hallucis brevis). Start seated with light hand pressure on your knee for Week 1 before doing standing single-leg towel raises.
