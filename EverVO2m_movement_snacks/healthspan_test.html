Why no single "biological age" score? Combining unequal tests into one number triggers Goodhart's Law: people optimise the composite (e.g., practising sit-and-reach) instead of improving the health domains that matter most. Push-up count and VO₂max carry far more mortality weight than flexibility, yet a composite would let a good stretch score mask a weak heart. Each test here stands on its own so you can focus your training where it counts.
